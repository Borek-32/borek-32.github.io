FPGA RC car: SPI, XADC & PWM interface

For my EE102 project, I built and integrated a two-Basys 3 control system that reads two joysticks through XADC, carries steering and throttle commands over an nRF24L01 radio link, and generates one steering and four ESC PWM outputs on the vehicle. The 2024 prototype remained responsive at approximately 500 m in a practical line-of-sight check, but the direct-drive motors could only turn the wheels with the chassis raised and the ESCs overheated under ground load. I rebuilt both FPGA bitstreams in 2026 with positive constrained slack, without a new board, radio, or motor test.

Project summary

what I built

Problem

Turn two-axis analog joystick movement into wireless steering and throttle commands for a four-wheel-drive vehicle using FPGA logic at both ends.

My contribution

I developed and integrated the XADC input path, eight-bit command format, register-level SPI radio control, receive-side handling, and PWM outputs with the handmade remote and vehicle hardware.

Result

Steering and throttle worked over the 2024 link, including a practical line-of-sight check at approximately 500 m. In 2026 the reconstructed remote and vehicle builds reached +1.178 ns and +3.675 ns WNS respectively, but were not retested on hardware.

Implementation flow

end-to-end control path
  1. Sample the joysticks.The handheld Basys3 alternates VAUX6 and VAUX14 through XADC and reduces each converter value to six control bits.
  2. Encode the command.REMOTE_UNIT converts steering or throttle movement into the 8-bit payload used by the project.
  3. Transmit over SPI radio.The transmitter-specific nrftop configures an NRF24L01, writes the payload, and controls the CE/CSN transaction sequence.
  4. Receive and normalize.The vehicle-specific nrftop reads the radio payload and passes it through nrf_errorhandler.
  5. Drive the chassis.CONTROL_UNIT routes the command into one steering channel and four motor/ESC PWM outputs.

Known limitations and next test

current status

What the result means

The 2024 distance was a practical observation, not a packet-loss, latency, or guaranteed-range measurement. The 2026 slack values cover constrained paths only: external I/O timing remains incomplete, 1,867 remote and 2,263 vehicle internal pins lack maximum-delay constraints, and SPI_MISO is false-pathed. The vehicle build also retains 12 inferred latches, and the reconstructed connector mapping has not been checked on hardware.

What I would test next

I would program both bitstreams, verify the reconstructed connector map, and record repeatable radio-command and steering tests. I would retest propulsion only with suitable geared motors and ESC protection, while monitoring current and temperature under load.
