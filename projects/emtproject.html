ULTRASONIC AUDIO / EVIDENCE DOCUMENT

EMT: ultrasonic directional-speaker prototype

A hardware/audio experiment in which MATLAB prepares and streams audio frames over UART to a Basys3 design. The FPGA decodes sample and steering data, then controls PWM/H-bridge output paths for an ultrasonic transducer array.

Executive summary

what was built

Problem

Explore narrow-region audio delivery through an ultrasonic transducer array while keeping the sample transport and steering control observable.

Implementation

MATLAB normalizes and quantizes audio, sends 64-sample frames plus a signed steering delta, and FPGA logic converts the decoded data into driver control.

Result

The documented build produced a prototype bitstream and a precise serial protocol; physical demo coverage and timing closure still need publication.

Implementation flow

audio path
  1. Prepare the audio.MATLAB reads a WAV file, converts it to mono, resamples, normalizes, and maps samples to unsigned 8-bit values.
  2. Calculate steering.The streamer derives signed deltaTicks from angle, element spacing, sound speed, and the FPGA clock.
  3. Transmit framed data.Each v2 frame carries a start marker, two steering bytes, 64 audio samples, and an end marker.
  4. Decode on FPGA.UART receive logic identifies frame fields and hands sample/steering values to the modulation and phase-control path.
  5. Drive transducers.Three H-bridge channel groups provide external control signals for the ultrasonic hardware.

Known limitations and next proof

honest status

Known limitation

The routed prototype has a documented setup-timing failure, likely around phase-offset paths. The available notes also do not yet establish a complete measured beam-steering result.

Best next evidence

Publish the design report, protocol capture, transducer/H-bridge wiring photo, timing summary, and a controlled before/after steering measurement or recording.
